Personal Finance Tracker

A private, read-only finance dashboard built and used by one person, chiyami, to see their own bank, investment and crypto accounts in one place.

What it does

  • Shows balances, transactions, holdings and investment activity from the owner's own accounts.
  • Runs only on the owner's own computer. It has no public users, no sign-ups and no public website.
  • Is read-only: it cannot move money, place trades or change anything at a bank or broker.

Data

Only the owner's own account data is read, with the owner's permission, and it stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else. See the Privacy Policy.
