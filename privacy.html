Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Personal Finance Tracker is a private tool used only by its owner, chiyami. It has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, through Plaid, the app reads the owner's own account names, balances, transactions, investment holdings and investment transactions.

How it is used

Only to display the owner's own finances on the owner's own computer. It is not used for advertising, profiling, lending decisions or any other purpose.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Access keys and tokens are kept in the Windows Credential Manager, never in files or on a website.
  • All connections use HTTPS. The app listens on the owner's computer only.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect an account at any time and delete the stored data and tokens from the computer.
